---
name: AI Guide purpose
description: Project purpose and learning experience constraints for the AI Guide workspace.
---

Treat the website and its Replit workspace as the user's personal learning tool for safely and securely learning AI and related systems. Keep it easy to navigate and suited to a beginner who learns by trying things.

**Why:** The user explicitly described the whole website and workspace this way.

**How to apply:** Use plain, step-by-step explanations and small practice activities. Clearly distinguish local examples from connected accounts, live services, or saved data.
